# T05 · The inspector
**Milestone:** M1 · **Reads:** `docs/05_UI_SPEC.md` §1

## Do
Sections in this order, **one expanded at a time**, count on every collapsed header:
1. **PROPERTIES** — the same typed rows, for when the centre pane holds a table.
2. **REFERENCED BY** — documents whose **frontmatter relation** points here. Show the pointing field name (`top_finds:`) and the source collection.
3. **LINKS** — `[[wikilinks]]` in the body, incoming and outgoing kept apart.
4. **HISTORY** — who changed what, agent or human, newest first, from the event log.
5. **GRAPH** — a small relation preview that opens **into the whiteboard** (T15). Not a second graph feature.

**REFERENCED BY and LINKS are different things and must look different.** The first is structured and typed; the second is prose.

## Demonstrate
A guest referenced by a territory, a learning and a project brief shows three entries with their field names.
